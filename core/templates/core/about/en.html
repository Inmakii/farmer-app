Farmer App

A digital farm notebook

You know what every field earned.

Farmer App is a free web app for farmers. You record fields, cultivations, works, sprayings and harvests, and the app works out costs, revenue and profit – for each field and each cultivation separately. Instead of a notebook and a calculator.

What you can do in the app

  • Fields on a mapName, area, soil and location: an address, a parcel number or a point on a map with an aerial photo.
  • Seasonal cultivationsWhat grows on a field this year, the sowing date and the planned harvest. Field history year by year.
  • Works and sprayingsPlowing, sowing, fertilizing and sprayings – with the date, product, dose and cost.
  • HarvestsHow much was harvested and what happened to it: sold, stored or lost.
  • ReportsCosts, revenue and profit of the farm, a field and a cultivation. You see at once which field pays off.
  • Crop catalogueOver 60 types: cereals, oilseeds, root crops, vegetables and more. Missing one? Add your own.

One season in Farmer App

  1. Springadd fields and cultivations, record plowing and sowing
  2. Summernote fertilizing and sprayings with their costs
  3. Autumnenter harvests: sold, stored, lost
  4. Winteropen the report and see the result of every field

Who it is for

  • small and medium farms,
  • farmers who now count in a notebook or in Excel,
  • students of agricultural schools.

How to start

  1. Go to the website and create a free account.
  2. Add your first field – a name and an area are enough.
  3. Record works and harvests, and open the report after the season.

Your data is safe. Only you see your fields and records. The connection is encrypted, and under the GDPR you can download your data or delete your account at any time.
